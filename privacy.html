Privacy Policy

Student Tools Hub · Effective 27 October 2026

In short
  • The notes, PDFs, images and text you give the app are sent to Google's AI (Gemini and Cloud Vision) to be read and processed. Giving the app your files is your choice, but the features cannot work without them.
  • Your information is not sold and not used to train AI. There are no ads, no analytics, and the site sets no cookies.
  • The service is free today, and you will never be charged without agreeing first (section 11).
  • Your notes stay in your own browser and, if you connect Google, in a hidden sync file in your own Google Drive. Short-lived copies are also kept on the app's services while it works: tutor chats for 3 hours, and file pointers for 47 hours. Error emails are kept for 90 days.
  • Error reports are sent to me automatically when something fails. The app's own reports hold the time, your browser details, two short messages and sometimes an error code (messages with email addresses, tokens, web addresses and file names removed where they can be recognised), with no stack traces or server error text. The server does not save a record of requests, so your files and text are not kept there. If the server itself fails, its error email names the step that failed and a short message (cut to 300 characters); I can see these (section 2).
  • Text read from scanned PDFs is kept for 30 days and then deleted by itself. It is filed under a key made from the file's fingerprint and a secret random key that lives only in your browser, not under your name, so nobody else can match it to a file, but the text itself may contain personal details.
  • The service is for people aged 18 and over, and the app asks you to confirm this with a tick box (section 10).
  • Signing out of sync revokes the Google permissions you gave the app and clears your sync details and lists of exported Docs from the browser (the list comes back from your sync file when you connect again), but deletes none of your notes or files. To see, correct or delete your information, email jayleigh.dev@gmail.com and follow the steps in section 8.
  • Connecting Google lets the app use only its own hidden folder and the files it creates itself (such as the Docs you export), not the rest of your Drive. Your Google access stays in your browser and is not sent to the server.

4. Google permissions and the Limited Use rule

When you connect Google, the app asks for the permissions below, and only uses them for the purpose shown. Export asks for Drive-file access; turning on sync asks for the hidden-folder, Drive-file, name and email access.

  • Drive files (drive.file, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): for the temporary conversion file and Docs you export: to create a new Google Doc when you export, to create the temporary conversion Doc for a legacy .doc file, and to add to a Doc this app created or one you choose with Google's file picker (it reads that Doc only to find where it ends and to read its title). Export asks for it, and so does turning on sync. It only covers files this app creates and the one Doc you choose in Google's file picker each time: Google gives the app access to that Doc only, the app learns its name and ID, keeps them in your recent-exports list and your sync file, and does not send them to the server. The app does not open any other Docs or files in your Drive. You can remove this access in your Google account's connections (myaccount.google.com/connections) or in Google Drive's settings. The contents of your Docs are not sent to the server.
  • Drive app data (drive.appdata, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): to keep the hidden sync file and copies of your scanned PDFs and legacy .doc files in your own Drive, so your work follows you between devices. Your browser also uses this access to download a scanned PDF that is stored only in Drive. This access stays in your browser and is not sent to the server.
  • Profile and email (userinfo.profile, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile; userinfo.email, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email): to show your name and picture and to know which account is connected.

Google user data is used only to provide these features. It is not sold and not used for advertising. Information obtained through Google Workspace APIs (Docs and Drive) is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI and/or ML models. I do not use what you upload to train any AI, and I have no way to do so. Google's Gemini service is on a paid plan where Google says it does not use prompts or responses to improve its products.

Your Google Docs and Drive data is not read by a person unless you ask for help, it is needed to investigate abuse, or the law requires it. Separately, the server's own error emails name the failed step with a short message (cut to 300 characters), and the hosting logs show time, address called, status and IP address; I may see those when I fix a fault. They are deleted as shown in section 2.

Student Tools Hub's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Keeping your information safe, and breaches

I take appropriate, reasonable steps, but no online service is completely secure and I cannot promise it is.

  • The app's pages and the Google services it uses are loaded over HTTPS. Web addresses you ask the server to read may start with http://, which is not encrypted, and the server may open web pages that are not properly secured.
  • Google sign-in uses Google's own system, so the app never sees your Google password.
  • The access code is a shared code that works as a simple gate. It is sent over HTTPS to the server when you sign in and compared there with a stored copy. The server then gives your browser a sign-in token, and every request to the server must carry that token. It is not strong protection: everyone who has the code can sign in, and the server does not know who is who. To limit guessing, the server counts sign-in attempts per network address (section 2).
  • The tutor's instructions are kept on the server. Your browser does not send them.
  • Tutor sessions, Gemini file pointers and scanned-PDF text expire on a timer (section 2). Their keys include a secret that stays in your browser, so other users and anyone reading the stored keys cannot find or reuse your entries from a file.
  • Your synced data sits in your own Google Drive, protected by your Google account. Use a strong password and 2-step verification.

Your browser keeps the sign-in token in its storage and uses it until it runs out, 7 days after you signed in (using the app does not extend it). The access code itself is not kept in your browser. After 7 days, or if the server stops accepting the token, you are asked for the code again. The token is not locked inside your browser, so anyone who can use your browser profile can use the app as you: do not use the app on a shared computer, and clear the site's data when you finish.

If there is a breach that puts your information at risk, I will tell the Information Regulator and the people affected as soon as reasonably possible. Because the app has no accounts and holds no contact list, I will do this through a notice on this page and, where I can, in the app.

  • Object to your information being used, or withdraw your consent. Withdrawing does not undo what was lawfully done before.

Email jayleigh.dev@gmail.com and say what you want. You may be asked to show who you are. I reply within 30 days. Rights requests are free of charge.

How to delete your data

Nothing is deleted automatically from your browser or Drive when you simply stop using the app. Cached copies on the server expire by themselves (see below).

  • On your device: clear this site's data in your browser. Signing out of sync in the app clears your sync details (your Google name, email and picture, the ID of your sync file and the two lists of Docs the app created or you picked, and Docs taken off that list) and revokes the Google permissions you gave the app (Drive files, hidden app data, name and email), including on your other devices. Next time you connect, Google asks for them again. Connecting to sync again brings the Docs list back from your sync file. If the app cannot remove them (for example Google cannot be reached or your sign-in has expired), or only some are removed, the app tells you, and the rest stay until you remove them at myaccount.google.com/connections. It does not clear the access code, your cache secret (section 14) or anything else in your browser.
  • Stop Google access: while you are connected to sync (the open side menu shows your name and a cross), signing out of sync in the app does this for you; if you only export to Docs, or the menu shows "Re-connect Google", use myaccount.google.com/connections, choose Student Tools Hub and remove its access. Syncing then stops; your hidden Drive data stays until you delete it as described below.
  • Your hidden Drive data: in Google Drive choose Settings → Manage apps → Student Tools Hub → Options → Delete hidden app data. Docs you exported, and a temporary __STH_CONVERT__ Doc that remained in your My Drive, are ordinary files in your Drive that you can delete.
  • On the server: tutor chats, Gemini file pointers (47 hours) and scanned-PDF text (30 days) expire by themselves. Hosting logs and error emails are deleted on the schedule in section 2. You can ask me to delete a specific error email or hosting log entry earlier: send the date and time. Scanned-PDF text and file pointers are filed under a key made with a secret that lives only in your browser, so neither I nor anyone else can find your entry from a file, and it cannot be deleted earlier on request; it simply expires. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes the secret, after which the entries can no longer be reached by anyone. If you have questions, email jayleigh.dev@gmail.com.
  • There is no "Delete my data" button in the app; use the steps above.
