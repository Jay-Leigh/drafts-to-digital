4. Google permissions and the Limited Use rule

When you connect Google, the app asks for the permissions below, and only uses them for the purpose shown. Export asks for Docs and Drive-file access; turning on sync asks for the hidden-folder, name and email access.

  • Google Docs (documents): to create a new Google Doc when you export. If you paste a link to one of your own Docs and choose to append, the app also reads that Doc to find where it ends and adds your text there. The app does not open any other Docs, and the contents of your Docs are not sent to the server.
  • Drive files (drive.file): requested together with Docs access when you export. It only covers files this app creates; the app does not use it to open any other file in your Drive.
  • Drive app data (drive.appdata): to keep the hidden sync file and copies of your scanned documents in your own Drive, so your work follows you between devices. When you process a scanned PDF that is not on this device, or a legacy .doc file, the server also uses this access to fetch that file from the hidden area, or to convert it there and delete the temporary copy.
  • Profile and email (userinfo.profile, userinfo.email): to show your name and picture and to know which account is connected.

Google user data is used only to provide these features. It is not sold and not used for advertising. Information obtained through Google Workspace APIs (Docs and Drive) is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI and/or ML models. Nobody reads it unless you give permission (for example, when you ask for help), where it is needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or where the law requires it.

Student Tools Hub's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Sending information outside South Africa

The app's server runs in Johannesburg, but Google may process your files and chats (Gemini, Cloud Vision, Drive, Gmail) outside South Africa, and Upstash stores its data in Cape Town, South Africa (AWS af-south-1). POPIA section 72 allows this where the receiving provider is bound by law, binding rules or an agreement that gives protection similar to POPIA. Google and Upstash publish data-protection terms. This policy does not treat your use of the app as consent to these transfers.

7. Keeping your information safe, and breaches

Reasonable steps are taken, but no online service is completely secure and I cannot promise it is.

  • The app's pages and the Google services it uses are loaded over HTTPS. Web addresses you ask the server to read may start with http://, which is not encrypted.
  • Google sign-in uses Google's own system, so the app never sees your Google password.
  • The access code is a shared code that works as a simple gate. It is not strong protection, and the server does not check who is sending each request.
  • Tutor sessions and Gemini file pointers expire on a timer (section 2). OCR text does not.
  • Your synced data sits in your own Google Drive, protected by your Google account. Use a strong password and 2-step verification.

The access code is stored in your browser for 24 hours, encoded but not encrypted. Do not use the app on a shared or public computer without signing out and clearing the site's data.

If there is a breach that puts your information at risk, I will tell the Information Regulator. Because the app has no accounts and holds no contact list, affected people will be told through a notice in the app and a notice at the top of this page.

8. Your rights and deleting your data

Under POPIA you can:

  • Ask what is held about you and get a copy.
  • Ask for corrections to anything wrong or out of date.
  • Ask for deletion, or for use to stop.
  • Object to your information being used, or withdraw your consent. Withdrawing does not undo what was lawfully done before.

Email jayleigh.dev@gmail.com and say what you want. You may be asked to show who you are. I reply within 30 days.

How to delete your data

  • On your device: clear this site's data in your browser. Signing out in the app clears only your Google account details used for sync, not the rest.
  • Stop Google access: go to myaccount.google.com/connections, choose Student Tools Hub and remove its access.
  • Your hidden Drive data: in Google Drive choose Settings → Manage apps → Student Tools Hub → Options → Delete hidden app data. Docs you exported are ordinary files in your Drive that you can delete.
  • On the server: tutor chats and Gemini file pointers expire by themselves. Execution logs and error emails are deleted on the schedule in section 2. For the OCR cache, email me the file or its SHA-256 hash, because entries are filed under the hash and not under your name. I remove those keys by hand.
  • Not built yet: a "Delete my data" button in the app. This page will be updated if it is added.

Browser storage

The site sets no cookies. Google Identity Services, Google Fonts and the other hosts in section 5 may use their own storage under their own policies. The app itself uses:

  • Local storage: the access code (base64-encoded) and when you unlocked, kept 24 hours; your Google name, email and picture for sync; the ID of your Drive sync file; whether the sidebar is collapsed.
  • IndexedDB: your draft in progress, 30 days of history, Knowledge Bay items (including images as base64), scanned-PDF progress, and tutor chats.

Both stay on your device until you clear the site's data. Signing out in the app clears only the Google account details.

Google Drive

The hidden sync file is sth-userdata-v1.json in the app data area (drive.appdata). It holds Knowledge Bay items, including base64 images and copies of scanned PDFs and documents, and tutor chats. During .doc conversion, temporary files are created in the app's hidden Drive area and deleted afterwards.
