Privacy Policy

Drafts to Digital (also called Student Tools Hub) · Effective 27 October 2026

The short version
  • The notes, PDFs and images you upload are sent to Google's AI (Gemini and Cloud Vision) so the app can read and process them.
  • If you connect Google, the app can create Google Docs for you and keep a hidden sync file in your own Google Drive.
  • Most of your saved work lives in your own browser. Some short-lived copies are kept on our servers while you work.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics.
  • If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree before you use the app.
  • You can ask us to show, fix or delete your information by emailing jayleigh.dev@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

The app is run by Jay-Leigh Vergotine in South Africa. Under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), she is the "responsible party": the person who decides why and how your personal information is used. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean her.

Contact: jayleigh.dev@gmail.com

3. Why we may use your information

  • Your consent when you use the features and connect your Google account. You can withdraw it at any time (section 7). If you are under 18, we also need your parent or guardian's consent (section 8).

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles of you.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app asks for these Google permissions, and only when you choose to use the feature:

  • Google Docs (documents) and Drive (drive.file): to create the Google Doc you export, and to open only files the app itself created.

Drafts to Digital's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps, but no online service is completely secure and we cannot promise it is.

  • All connections to the app and its providers use HTTPS.
  • The app is locked behind an access code, and its back-end addresses and keys are kept out of the page's readable code.
  • Google sign-in uses Google's own system, so we never see your Google password. The Google access token is kept in the page's memory and is not saved to your browser storage.
  • Chat sessions and file pointers are deleted automatically on a timer (section 2).
  • Your synced data sits in your own Google Drive, protected by your Google account. Use a strong password and 2-step verification.

Please be aware: the access code is stored in your browser for 24 hours so you stay unlocked, and it is not encrypted there. Do not use the app on a shared or public computer without signing out and clearing your browser data. [CONFIRM: owner to decide whether to keep this sentence or fix the storage method in the app]

If a security breach affects your information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

6. Browser storage and cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app keeps data in your browser's storage so it works without our servers holding your files:

  • Local storage: your access code and when you unlocked (24 hours); the name, email and picture of your connected Google account; the ID of your Drive sync file; whether the sidebar is collapsed.
  • Browser database (IndexedDB): your draft in progress, your recent history (kept for 30 days), your Knowledge Bay documents and text read from them, scanned-PDF progress, and your tutor chats.

This stays on your device until you delete it, sign out of sync (which clears the Google account details), or clear your browser's site data. Google Sign-In, Google Fonts and the other hosts in section 4 may set their own cookies or storage under their own policies. 7. Your rights and how to use them

Under POPIA you can:

  • Ask what we hold about you and get a copy (access).
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Ask us to delete your information, or to stop using it.
  • Object to us using your information, or withdraw your consent. Withdrawing does not undo what we already did lawfully before you withdrew.

To use any of these, email jayleigh.dev@gmail.com from the address you use with the app and say what you want. We may ask you to prove who you are. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Deleting your data

  • Now, yourself: clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything stored on your device. In Google, go to your Google Account → Data & privacy → third-party apps, remove Drafts to Digital, and delete its hidden app data. Google Docs you exported are ordinary Docs in your Drive that you can delete.
  • On request: email us and we will delete the chat sessions and cached content we can find that relate to you in our systems. Because some cache entries are filed under a file fingerprint, not your name, we may need you to tell us which files you used.
  • Planned: a "Delete my data" button inside the app is planned but does not exist yet. We will update this policy when it launches.
  • What stays: error reports kept for diagnostics will be anonymised where possible rather than deleted one by one, because they do not carry your name or email.

Complaints

If you think we have handled your information wrongly, please talk to us first. You can also complain to South Africa's Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

8. Children and teenagers

The app is for students aged 13 and older. If you are under 13, please do not use it.

Under POPIA, anyone under 18 is a child. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to you using the app and to your information being processed as described here before you start. By letting you use the app, your parent or guardian confirms they have read this policy. A parent or guardian can email us at any time to see, correct or delete their child's information or withdraw consent.

We do not knowingly use children's information for marketing. If we learn that someone under 13 has used the app, we will delete what we hold about them.
